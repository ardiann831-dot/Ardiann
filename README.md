# 19 November World News — Visual Summary (`news-image-20261119`)

A high-density editorial infographic broadsheet summarizing global themes and developments for November 19 in a single image.

## Visual Coverage
- **International Diplomacy:** Global summit globes, regional alliances, and geopolitical coordination maps.
- **Global Headlines:** Structured summary cards covering climate talks, economic agreements, and trade deals.
- **Energy Transition:** Clean energy grid networks, solar capacity growth, and power distribution metrics.
- **Financial Markets:** Multi-panel market trends, currency movements, and equity performance indicators.
- **Maritime Shipping Lanes:** Global trade route density and shipping corridor analytics.
- **Technology Infrastructure:** Data center distribution, AI network topology, and cybersecurity overviews.
- **Space Exploration:** Orbital tracks, satellite deployments, and mission milestones.

## Image Generation Prompt
> Dense editorial information broadsheet poster titled "19 NOVEMBER WORLD NEWS" with multiple interconnected news panels, diplomatic summit globes, financial market graphs, clean energy grid maps, maritime shipping lanes, space orbital tracks, and tech infrastructure diagrams, packed with crisp visual icons, data charts, regional badges, and structured callout boxes in a high-contrast modern infographic style.
